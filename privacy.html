Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Scoreboard Last Updated: November 10, 2026 Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data Scoreboard ("the Bot") collects, how it is used, and how you can request its deletion. 1. Information We Collect To provide reading tracking and leaderboard rankings, the Bot collects and stores only the minimum data required to function: • Discord User ID: A unique numerical identifier used to link your reading progress to your account. • Discord Username: Used to display your name on the public server leaderboard and check-in receipts. • Activity Metrics: The number of pages logged via /checkin, calculated point totals, and timestamps of your most recent check-in (used solely to calculate daily streaks and inactivity deductions). We do not collect message histories outside of slash command inputs, passwords, email addresses, IP addresses, or private direct messages. 2. How We Use Your Information Collected data is used exclusively to: • Process /checkin submissions and award base or speed-bonus points. • Apply automated midnight UTC point adjustments for missed reading days. • Render and update the public paginated leaderboard within your Discord server. 3. Data Storage and Security All user records are stored securely in encrypted Cloudflare Workers KV storage. Data is transmitted strictly over encrypted HTTPS connections using Discord's official Interactions API. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. 4. Data Retention and Deletion Your leaderboard profile remains stored for the duration of your participation in the reading challenge. You may request the complete deletion of your stored profile and score history at any time by contacting hmnmnmm. or emailing gaeba88@gmail.com. Upon request, your record will be permanently purged from our database within 7 days. 5. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in bot functionality. Continued use of the Bot after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
